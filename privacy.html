1. Who we are

Mechanic Hub is a trading name of [COMPANY LEGAL NAME], registration number [REGISTRATION NUMBER], based at [BUSINESS ADDRESS], South Africa ("Mechanic Hub", "we", "us"). We provide recruitment services that connect automotive technicians with automotive businesses in the United States. We are the responsible party (data controller) for the personal information described in this policy.

2. Information we collect

From businesses (hiring inquiry form)

  • Contact details: name, business name, work email, phone number, business website.
  • Business details: city, state, number of locations, shop type.
  • Role details: technician level and number needed, position description, pay range, timeline, openness to nationwide or relocation candidates, and anything else you choose to tell us.

From technicians (application form)

  • Contact details: name, email, phone, city, state, LinkedIn profile.
  • Professional details: years of experience, technician level, employment status, areas of expertise, certifications, makes and models, diagnostic equipment experience, approximate tool value, repair capability, preferred locations, desired compensation, relocation preferences, availability, and your résumé if you upload one.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, bank details, driver's license numbers or similar government identifiers through this website. Please don't include them in free-text fields or résumés you send us.

Collected automatically

When you submit a form, our server records the date and time, your IP address and browser user-agent, and which consent boxes you checked. We use this for security, spam prevention and to keep a record of consent. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to hiring inquiries and provide our recruitment services.
  • To assess technicians for current and future roles and match them with suitable employers.
  • To contact you about your inquiry, application or relevant opportunities.
  • To run recruitment advertising campaigns on behalf of clients, with their authorization.
  • To maintain records, prevent fraud and spam, and meet legal obligations.

We will only share a technician's profile with a prospective employer after discussing the specific opportunity with that technician. We may use automated tools, including AI-assisted tools, to help organize and pre-screen applications. Hiring decisions are made by employers, not by automated systems, and you can ask us for a human review of any screening outcome.

4. Phone calls and text messages

Submitting a form allows us to contact you by email and by manually dialed phone call about that inquiry or application. We will only make calls or send texts using automated technology, or using an artificial or prerecorded voice, if you separately check the optional consent box for that purpose. That consent is not a condition of using our services. You can withdraw it at any time by replying STOP to a text, asking us during a call, or emailing [CONTACT EMAIL]. We do not use your form submission as consent for unrelated marketing.

5. Who we share information with

  • Prospective employers: technician details, once discussed with the technician.
  • Candidates: relevant role and business details, so they can consider the opportunity.
  • Service providers who process data for us under contract, such as website hosting ([HOSTING PROVIDER]), our CRM (GoHighLevel, once connected), email delivery ([EMAIL PROVIDER]) and font delivery (Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when pages load).
  • Advertising platforms: we manage recruitment campaigns on Meta platforms inside our clients' own ad accounts. Meta's handling of data is governed by Meta's own policies.
  • Authorities where required by law.

We do not sell personal information.

6. International transfers

We operate from South Africa and our clients and candidates are in the United States. Your information will be processed in South Africa, the United States and wherever our service providers host data. We take reasonable steps, including contractual protections, to ensure an adequate level of protection for information transferred across borders.

7. How long we keep information

Technician profiles are kept for up to [24] months after your last contact with us, so we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Client inquiry and engagement records are kept for as long as needed to provide our services and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, typically [5] years. Consent records are kept as long as needed to demonstrate compliance.

8. Security

Forms are submitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Uploaded résumés are stored outside the public website and are not publicly accessible. Access is limited to people who need it to deliver our services. No system is completely secure, and we will notify affected people and regulators of a security compromise where the law requires.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent, and lodge a complaint with a regulator. In South Africa, that regulator is the Information Regulator. Some U.S. states give residents similar rights. To make a request, email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

10. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and the forms do not set cookies. If we add analytics, advertising pixels or similar tools in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

11. Children

Our services are for adults seeking or offering employment. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.
