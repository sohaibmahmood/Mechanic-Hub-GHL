Who we recruit

Three technician levels. One specialty.

We recruit automotive technicians only, matched to the level your shop actually needs.

MTop tier

Master Technicians

Highly experienced professionals with advanced diagnostic and repair capability. The people your other techs go to when a job has them stuck.

  • Advanced diagnostics across electrical, drivability and network faults
  • Deep experience on complex and repeat-comeback repairs
  • Often the hardest level to find, so searches can take longer
ASenior

A-Level Technicians

Highly skilled, experienced technicians who handle complex diagnostic work and a broad range of repairs.

  • Typically 7+ years of hands-on experience
  • Often a substantial personal tool investment, commonly $30,000 or more
  • Broad bumper-to-bumper repair capability
BCore

B-Level Technicians

Competent technicians who carry general repair and maintenance work and a defined range of diagnostics.

  • General repair and scheduled maintenance
  • Diagnostics within a defined scope
  • A solid fit for growing bays and high-volume shops

These are general indicators, not automatic qualifications. Every candidate is assessed on their actual experience, technical competence, diagnostic ability, tools and relevant certifications against the requirements of your specific role.

How it works

Six steps from open role to new hire.

A structured process so you know what's happening at every stage.

  1. Intake

    Understand the shop

    We learn your business, location, open role, technician level, pay range, experience, certification and tool expectations.

  2. Campaign

    Launch recruitment campaigns

    With your authorization, we run targeted recruitment campaigns through your own Meta ad account. You pay Meta directly for ad spend.

  3. Screening

    We review applicants against your requirements. Screening may cover work history, technical skills, diagnostic experience, certifications and tool ownership.

    We introduce suitable candidates to you for further evaluation and interviews, with notes on how each matches your brief.

  5. Decision

    Support your hiring decision

    You interview and you decide. We coordinate between you and the candidate to keep the process moving.

  6. Guarantee

    30-day replacement guarantee

    If a placement doesn't work out in the first 30 days, we run a replacement search with no additional recruitment fee, under the written guarantee terms.

FAQ

Straight answers.

For shops and dealerships
What does the $8,000 fee cover?

One recruitment search: intake and role brief, setup and management of the agreed recruitment campaign in your Meta ad account, candidate sourcing and screening, a shortlist of suitable candidates, coordination through your hiring decision, and the 30-day replacement guarantee under your written agreement.

Why is the fee paid upfront?

The work starts the moment you sign: building the role brief, configuring campaigns and screening applicants. An upfront fee lets us commit that time to your search. You know the full recruitment cost before anything begins.

How much do we need to spend on Meta ads?

It depends on your market, the technician level and how competitive the role is. We agree on a budget with you before any campaign launches. Ad spend is billed by Meta directly to the payment method on your own ad account, and it is separate from the recruitment fee.

We need more than one technician. How is that priced?

The fee is $8,000 per recruitment search engagement. If you need several technicians, we'll scope it with you and confirm in writing how many engagements apply before you pay anything.

Do the technicians work for Mechanic Hub?

No. This is permanent, direct-hire recruitment. You interview the candidates, make the hiring decision and employ the technician directly.

How long will it take to fill our role?

A local search typically takes around 7 to 60 days. Location, technician level, pay, your requirements and candidate availability all affect timing. Master Technician roles and hard-to-hire areas often take longer. We can't promise a role will be filled within a set number of days.

Do you guarantee that we'll make a hire?

No recruiter can honestly guarantee that. We guarantee a structured process: campaigns aimed at your role, screening against your requirements and a shortlist of suitable candidates. The hiring decision is always yours.

How does the 30-day replacement guarantee work?

If a placed technician leaves or doesn't work out in the first 30 days, we run a replacement search with no additional recruitment fee, subject to the written guarantee terms in your service agreement. It's a replacement service, not a refund. Ad spend paid to Meta isn't refunded, and a replacement can't be promised immediately.

Can you recruit technicians from other states?

Yes. If local talent is limited, we can run a nationwide search and help you build a relocation offer. Whether it works depends on the candidate's willingness, the offer and the practical arrangements, so relocation can't fill every role.

Do you provide visa sponsorship or check work authorization?

We don't provide immigration sponsorship and we don't guarantee work authorization. Verifying a new hire's employment eligibility remains the employer's responsibility.

You're based in South Africa. How does that work for a U.S. shop?

Our team operates from South Africa and works with shops across the United States. Campaigns run in your own Meta account targeting your area, and we schedule calls at times that suit your time zone.
